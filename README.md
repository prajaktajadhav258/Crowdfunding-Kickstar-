# Crowdfunding-Kickstar-
"This dashboard analyzes Kickstarter crowdfunding campaigns to understand project performance, funding trends, campaign outcomes, and category-wise distribution. The goal is to help creators and stakeholders identify the factors that influence a campaign's success and make better funding decisions."

At the top, I have included six KPI cards that provide an overview of the entire dataset."

Total Projects: 365,892 campaigns analyzed.
Total Categories: 169 different project categories.
Average Campaign Duration: 79 days.
Success Rate: 38.35%, meaning about 38 out of every 100 campaigns were successful.
Total Goal Amount: $213.72 Million.
Total Backers: 45 Million supporters participated across all campaigns.

"I added interactive slicers for Year, Country, and Project State. These allow users to filter the dashboard dynamically and analyze specific periods, countries, or campaign outcomes."
